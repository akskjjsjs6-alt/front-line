# Map data credits

- **Historical borders** (`world-1900.json`, `world-1938.json`, `world-2000.json`) are derived from
  [historical-basemaps](https://github.com/aourednik/historical-basemaps) by André Ourednik and contributors,
  licensed under the **GNU General Public License v3.0**. The derived files in this folder are therefore
  also distributed under GPL-3.0. They were rasterised, split into provinces and assigned to empires by
  `tools/build_map.py`; the 1900 African colonial borders are filled in from the same project's 1914 map.
- **City names** used to name provinces come from [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/)
  (`ne_50m_populated_places_simple`), which is in the public domain.

Borders are approximate and simplified for gameplay.
