Front Line — your own AI

The game's AI game master can run on the actual Gemini web chat you already have — no accounts, no API keys, no paying anyone. This page installs the tiny helper that lets the game and your Gemini tab talk to each other.

The game and gemini.google.com are different sites, so a browser page isn't allowed to type into another site's tab — that's the wall. The extension is a ~100-line helper that bridges your game tab and your Gemini tab. It runs only inside those two tabs, sends nothing anywhere except between them, and needs no permissions on any other site.
  1. Download the extension: front-line-relay-ai.zip — and unzip it somewhere permanent (like Documents).
  2. Open chrome://extensions in Chrome or Edge, switch on Developer mode (top-right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped front-relay folder.
  4. In Front Line: tap the AI badge in-game, choose “My AI, no account (paste mode)”, and connect. From now on the game writes its prompt straight into your logged-in Gemini tab and reads the answer back — you only watch Gemini think in your own tab.

Keep a Gemini tab (or Chrome window) open while playing — the relay reuses any open gemini.google.com tab, or opens one for you. Everything falls back to the manual paste sheet, and to built-in rules, if anything goes wrong.
